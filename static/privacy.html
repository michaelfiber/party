Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Party Cam (cubeofb.org/party) turns guests' phones into cameras for a party. This page explains what we collect, why, who it goes to, and how to delete it. If anything here is unclear, email admin@cubeofb.org.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run the party: the host's phone number, the party details, the photos guests take, and the cloud connection the host sets up.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Photos are kept on our server until the host deletes the party. Deleting it removes the party and its photos from our server right away.

What we collect

If you host a party

  • Phone number. We text a login code to it, and we use it to link your parties to you so only you can open them.
  • Party details. The party's name and when it was created.
  • Cloud connection. If you connect Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive, we store the access tokens that service gives us, your account's name or email, and the folder you pick. We use them only to list and create folders in that service and to save the party's photos into your chosen folder.
  • Phone numbers you text the guest link to. If you use "Text the guest link", we send that one text and don't store the number.

If you're a guest

  • Your photos. Each photo you take is sent to our server, along with the time and which camera (front or back) took it. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or number.
  • Camera access. Your browser asks before the camera turns on. The live view stays on your phone. Only the photos you take are sent.

For everyone

  • Technical logs. Like most websites, our server records basic request information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. We use it to keep the service running and to stop abuse. The operator may also get a short alert when a party is created or a cloud account is connected. The alert can include an IP address or the connected account's name.

Who can see the photos

  • The host, on the party page.
  • Anyone the host gives the slideshow link or shows the slideshow to. The host can turn the old link off at any time with "Make a new link".
  • Anyone with access to the host's cloud folder, once the photos are copied there.

Guests' camera links let people add photos. They don't let anyone browse the album.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the services that make Party Cam work:

  • Twilio, to send text messages (login codes and guest links).
  • Google, Dropbox, or Microsoft, only if the host connects that service, to save photos into the host's own account.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the site's typefaces. Your browser contacts Google to load them, so Google can see your IP address.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Google user data

When a host connects Google Drive, Google's permission screen asks for access to Drive so you can choose any folder. Party Cam uses that access only to list your folders, create a folder if you ask, and upload the party's photos. It also reads your account name and email to show which account is connected. We don't read, change, or delete your other files. Party Cam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and storage

We use only the cookies and browser storage the site needs to work: one to keep a host logged in on that device, and one to remember which party is open. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. Logging out clears the login from that device.

How long we keep things, and deleting them

  • Photos and party details stay on our server until the host deletes the party.
  • Deleting a party. On "Your parties", tap Delete next to a party. This immediately removes from our server the party, every photo taken at it, and any cloud tokens stored with it. Its guest QR code and slideshow link stop working.
  • Your cloud folder is yours. Deleting a party doesn't remove copies already saved to your Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive folder. Delete those in that service. You can also remove Party Cam's access in your Google, Dropbox, or Microsoft account settings at any time.
  • Login codes expire after 10 minutes. A login stays active on a device until you log out.
  • Server logs are kept only as long as needed to run and secure the service.

Guests who want a photo of themselves removed can ask the host, or email us and we'll help.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email admin@cubeofb.org, including the phone number you use with Party Cam if you're a host, and we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Security

Pages and photos are sent over HTTPS. Party links use long random codes, and only the logged-in host can open a party. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't use Party Cam for photos you couldn't stand to have exposed.

Children

Party Cam isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Hosts shouldn't create accounts for children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll make them clear on the site.

Contact

Questions or requests: admin@cubeofb.org
